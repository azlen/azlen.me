*Just general concepts from various fields that I find particularly interesting or alluring to use as metaphors. Or things I've thought would make good metaphors. Not necessarily ones that have entered my personal [Lexicon](/lexicon).*

Countergradient
~ Adaptation that opposes environmental gradients

Metastability
~ Seemingly stable state vulnerable to sudden transition

Palimpsest
~ Text written over earlier, partially erased writing; maybe something that takes the place of the old?
